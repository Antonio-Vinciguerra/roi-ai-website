Heading South / Privacy Notice

Privacy, made clear.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This notice explains how Heading South Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit headingsouth.ai, use our AI advisor, submit an enquiry, or request a call.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Heading South Ltd
Registered address: 28A Park Road, Wallington, Surrey, United Kingdom
Privacy contact: info@headingsouth.ai

2. Information we collect

We may collect your name, work email, telephone number, company name, role, website, information submitted through our forms or advisor, callback preferences, time zone, language, notes, and basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it.

3. How we use your information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange requested calls or support, operate and improve our services, maintain relevant CRM records, protect our systems, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal basis

We use personal information only where there is a valid legal basis. This may include steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our business, consent where required, and legal obligations.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@headingsouth.ai.

5. AI advisor and callback requests

Our website may use an AI advisor to provide general information and guide visitors to relevant next steps. If you request a callback, we use your contact details to arrange and make that call.

Where we use a voice assistant, the call begins with clear notice that an AI assistant is involved. Calls may be recorded or transcribed for quality assurance, accurate follow-up and customer support. You may end the call at any time or ask for a human follow-up.

6. Service providers

We use selected providers to operate our services, including Cloudflare, HubSpot, n8n, Twilio, ElevenLabs and OpenAI, where enabled. They process information only as needed to provide services to us and under appropriate safeguards.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where required, we use appropriate legal safeguards for those transfers.

8. Retention

Callback requests and related call records, transcripts or recordings are retained for up to 30 days from collection, then deleted or anonymised, unless a longer period is required to resolve a support issue or comply with a legal obligation. Security logs are retained only as long as necessary for security and troubleshooting.

9. Your rights

You may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to use of your information, withdraw consent, and request portability where applicable. To make a request, email info@headingsouth.ai. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Cookies and analytics

We use necessary technologies to operate and secure the website. Where non-essential analytics, advertising or similar technologies are enabled, we will provide clear information and seek consent where required.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
